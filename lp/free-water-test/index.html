Whole-house filtration

Black Diamond Carbon Filter

A deep bed of premium activated carbon treats water as it enters your home, so every tap, shower and appliance gets cleaner water.

  • Reduces chlorine, chloramine, taste, odor and many organics
  • Choice of coconut shell or catalytic carbon media
  • Sized for your home: 1.0, 1.5 or 2.0 cubic feet
  • No salt and no added chemicals

Seeing scale on faucets or spots on dishes? We also install the Diamond Soft metered water softener, which removes the calcium and magnesium behind hard-water buildup.

Drinking water

Elevated Drinking System

Five-stage reverse osmosis that purifies your drinking water, then adds healthy minerals back for better taste.

  1. Sediment pre-filter captures sand, silt and rust down to 5 microns.
  2. Carbon block reduces chlorine, taste and odor.
  3. Reverse osmosis membrane removes dissolved contaminants down to 0.0001 micron.
  4. Remineralization filter adds calcium and magnesium back and balances pH.
  5. Polishing carbon gives a final pass for crisp, clean taste.

Questions before you book

Is the water test really free?

Yes. The in-home test and the written report are free, and there's no obligation to buy anything afterward.

How long does it take?

Plan on about an hour. That covers the testing in your home and the walkthrough of your results.

Do I need to be home?

Yes. The technician tests the water in your home and goes over the report with you, so an adult should be there for the visit.

Which areas do you serve?

We have local teams in the Phoenix metro, South Florida (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, plus Fort Myers, Cape Coral and Naples) and the Orlando metro, including Kissimmee and the Space Coast.

What happens after I send the form?

A member of your local team contacts you by phone to pick a day and time for your in-home test. Prefer to talk now? Call (480) 690-0600.

Will someone try to sell me a system?

Your technician explains what the report shows and, if your water needs it, what would fix it and what it costs. There are no pushy sales tactics and no hidden fees. The decision is yours.

Does the test cover my whole home or just my drinking water?

Whole-home concerns like hardness and chlorine affect showers, laundry and appliances as well as drinking water, and your technician looks at both. The report explains which fix fits which problem.

What does upkeep look like for the Elevated Drinking System?

The quick-change filters are replaced about once a year and the membrane about every two years. It uses a quiet, non-electric permeate pump and a 3-gallon storage tank so water is ready on demand.

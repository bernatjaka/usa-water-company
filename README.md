# USA Water Company

Marketing website for USA Water Company — professional water filtration, softening,
and certified water testing serving the Phoenix, AZ and South Florida (Miami) metros.

## Pages
- `index.html` — homepage (services, how it works, reviews, service areas, contact)
- `products.html` — water treatment product catalog
- `phoenix.html` — Phoenix, AZ service area page
- `south-florida.html` — South Florida / Miami service area page

## Assets
- `styles.css` — site styles
- `logo.jpg` — company logo
- `phoenix-cityscape.jpg`, `miami-skyline.jpg` — service-area hero photos

## Running locally
Open `index.html` in any web browser. No build step or dependencies required.

## Notes
- The contact/quote forms currently show a confirmation popup only; wire them to a
  form service (Formspree, Netlify Forms, etc.) before launch.
- The Miami hero photo is high resolution and should be compressed before launch.

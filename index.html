Black Diamond Carbon Filter

Water Softener

Diamond Soft Water Softener

A metered water softener that removes the calcium and magnesium behind hard-water scale, protecting your home and leaving your water noticeably softer.

Say Goodbye to Hard-Water Scale

Hard water leaves scale on fixtures, spots on dishes, and shortens the life of your appliances. The Diamond Soft softener uses ion exchange to remove those minerals, so you get softer skin and hair, cleaner dishes, and longer-lasting plumbing and appliances.

  • Available in 30,000, 45,000, and 60,000 grain capacities
  • Efficient metered control valve that regenerates only when needed
  • Protects water heaters, fixtures, and appliances from scale
  • Includes a separate brine tank
  • Backed by a lifetime warranty

Drinking Water

Elevated Drinking System

A five-stage reverse osmosis system that purifies your water, then adds healthy minerals back for cleaner, better-tasting water at the tap.

Five Stages of Purification

  1. Sediment pre-filter. Captures sand, silt, and rust down to 5 microns.
  2. Carbon block. Reduces chlorine, taste, and odor.
  3. Reverse osmosis membrane. Removes dissolved contaminants down to 0.0001 micron.
  4. Remineralization filter. Adds calcium and magnesium back and balances pH.
  5. Polishing carbon post-filter. A final pass for crisp, clean taste.
  • Quiet, water-efficient non-electric permeate pump
  • 3-gallon storage tank for water on demand
  • Quick-change filters (about yearly) and membrane (about every two years)
  • Great for drinking, coffee, tea, and ice

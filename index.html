HARDNESS

10.4gpgVery hard

0Soft below 3.5 gpg14 gpg

On the USGS scale, water above about 10.5 grains per gallon is very hard. Hardness is what leaves white scale on faucets and shower heads, and what shortens the life of a water heater.

Reference: USGS hardness scale

CHLORINE

1.4ppmWithin limits

0EPA limit 4.0 ppm4 ppm

The EPA allows up to 4.0 parts per million of chlorine in drinking water. It is there to keep the water safe on the way to your house. Most people notice it as taste or smell at the tap.

Reference: EPA disinfectant limits

IRON

0.18ppmWithin limits

0Staining above 0.3 ppm0.6 ppm

The EPA secondary standard for iron is 0.3 parts per million. Above that you start to see orange staining in sinks, tubs and laundry, and a metallic taste in the water.

Reference: EPA secondary standards

PH

7.6Ideal range

6Ideal 6.5 to 8.59

The EPA recommends drinking water sits between 6.5 and 8.5. Below that range water turns corrosive on your pipes. Above it you get scale and a flat taste.

Reference: EPA secondary standards

TOTAL DISSOLVED SOLIDS

420ppmWithin limits

0EPA guidance 500 ppm1000 ppm

Total dissolved solids is everything that would be left behind if the water evaporated: minerals, salts and metals. The EPA secondary standard is 500 parts per million, mostly for taste.

Reference: EPA secondary standards

Water Softener

Diamond Soft Water Softener

A metered water softener that removes the calcium and magnesium causing hard-water scale, protecting your plumbing and appliances while leaving your water noticeably softer.

Say Goodbye to Hard-Water Scale

Hard water leaves scale on fixtures, spots on dishes, and shortens the life of your appliances. The Diamond Soft softener uses ion exchange to remove those minerals, so you get softer skin and hair, cleaner dishes, and longer-lasting plumbing and appliances.

  • Available in 30,000, 45,000, and 60,000 grain capacities
  • Efficient metered control valve that regenerates only when needed
  • Protects water heaters, fixtures, and appliances from scale

Drinking Water

Elevated Drinking System

A five-stage reverse osmosis system that purifies your water, then adds healthy minerals back for cleaner, better-tasting water at the tap.

Five Stages of Purification

  1. Sediment pre-filter. Captures sand, silt, and rust down to 5 microns.
  2. Carbon block. Reduces chlorine, taste, and odor.
  3. Reverse osmosis membrane. Removes dissolved contaminants down to 0.0001 micron.
  4. Remineralization filter. Adds calcium and magnesium back and balances pH.
  5. Polishing carbon post-filter. A final pass for crisp, clean taste.
  • Quiet, water-efficient non-electric permeate pump
  • 3-gallon storage tank for water on demand
  • Quick-change filters (about yearly) and membrane (about every two years)
  • Great for drinking, coffee, tea, and ice

Questions about your free water test

Is the water test really free?

Yes. The in-home test, the written report and the walkthrough cost nothing, and there is no obligation to buy anything afterwards. If you decide a system is not for you, you keep the report either way.

How long does the visit take?

About an hour. A technician tests the water at your tap, goes through each reading with you in plain language, and answers whatever you want to ask.

What do you actually test for?

Hardness, chlorine, iron, pH and total dissolved solids are measured at your tap during the visit. If you have a specific concern such as lead or PFAS, tell us when you book and we will arrange a certified lab test.

Which areas do you cover?

Phoenix and the wider Valley, South Florida from Miami-Dade up through Palm Beach plus Fort Myers, Cape Coral and Naples, and Orlando out to the Space Coast. Enter your zip when you book and you are routed to the local team.

Who installs the system if I buy one?

Every system is professionally installed by licensed contractors. Installation is included in the quoted price.

Do I have to be home for the test?

Yes, someone over 18 needs to be there, since the technician tests the water at your own taps and goes through the results with you in person.

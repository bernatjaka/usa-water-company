About Phoenix Water

Some of the Hardest Water in the Country

The Phoenix metro is well known for very hard water. Supplies drawn from the Colorado River, the Salt River, and local groundwater carry high levels of calcium and magnesium, often measuring well above the national average.

That hardness is what leaves white scale on your faucets and glassware, shortens the life of water heaters and appliances, and can leave skin and hair feeling dry after a shower. Municipal water is also treated with chlorine, which affects taste and smell.

A free in-home test tells you exactly what is in your water, and we build a solution around your home, your water source, and your budget.

  • Whole-home softening for hard-water scale
  • Filtration for chlorine taste and odor
  • Reverse osmosis for clean drinking water
  • Certified testing with a written report

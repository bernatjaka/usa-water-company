Water Softener Installation

Hard water carries dissolved calcium and magnesium. Phoenix has some of the hardest tap water in the country, and the Florida aquifers are limestone, which is where hardness comes from.

That mineral content is what leaves scale on fixtures, spots on dishes, stiff laundry and buildup inside a water heater.

A properly sized softener addresses the minerals responsible. We measure your home’s actual hardness with a free in-home test and size the equipment to that number, rather than selling one size to everybody.

What this system addresses

Scale on fixtures

The white, crusty deposits on faucets, shower heads and glass doors are hardness minerals left behind as water evaporates. Softening addresses them at the source.

Water heater buildup

Hardness minerals precipitate inside a water heater as scale. The Department of Energy lists scale buildup as a cause of reduced heating efficiency.

Spots on dishes

Cloudy film and spots on glassware after the dishwasher are mineral residue left behind as the water dries, not a detergent problem.

Soap and lather

Hardness minerals react with soap to form curd instead of lather, which is why soap behaves so differently in soft water.

Dry skin and hair

Households in hard water areas commonly report skin feeling dry and hair feeling dull after bathing. Your test confirms whether hardness is actually present at your address.

Laundry and appliance wear

Mineral deposits accumulate in washing machines and dishwashers over time. Treating hardness at the point of entry addresses the source rather than the symptom.

Signs to watch for

  • White crust on faucets, shower heads or the kettle
  • Spots and cloudy film on glassware straight out of the dishwasher
  • Soap and shampoo that barely lather
  • Laundry coming out stiff or grey
  • A water heater that is not lasting as long as it should

About South Florida Water

Hard Water From the Limestone Aquifer

Most of South Florida draws its water from the Biscayne Aquifer, a shallow layer of porous limestone. As water moves through that limestone it picks up calcium and other minerals, which makes the region's water naturally hard.

Homeowners here often notice scale on fixtures, spotting on dishes, and appliances that wear out faster. Well water in the area can also carry a sulfur smell and iron staining, while municipal supplies are treated with chlorine that affects taste and odor.

A free in-home test tells you exactly what is in your water, and we build a solution around your home, your water source, and your budget.

  • Whole-home softening for hard-water scale
  • Filtration for chlorine, sulfur, and iron
  • Reverse osmosis for clean drinking water
  • Certified testing with a written report

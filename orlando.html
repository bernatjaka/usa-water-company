About Orlando Water

Hard Water From the Floridan Aquifer

Central Florida draws most of its water from the Floridan Aquifer, a deep layer of limestone. As water moves through that rock it picks up calcium and other minerals, which makes Orlando-area water naturally hard.

Homeowners here often notice scale on fixtures, spotting on dishes, and appliances that wear out faster. Some wells also carry a sulfur smell from hydrogen sulfide, while municipal supplies are treated with chlorine that affects taste and odor.

A free in-home test tells you exactly what is in your water, and we build a solution around your home, your water source, and your budget.

  • Whole-home softening for hard-water scale
  • Filtration for chlorine, sulfur, and sediment
  • Elevated drinking water for clean, great-tasting water
  • Certified testing with a written report

Whole-House Filtration

Whole-House Iron & Sulfur Filter

A whole-house system built for well water that removes iron, rust, sulfur odor, and sediment using natural air filtration, with no salt and no chemicals.

A Four-Stage, Chemical-Free Cycle

  1. In service. Water enters and iron, manganese, and sulfur are trapped in the natural media while oxygen is added.
  2. Backwash. An upward flow lifts the filter bed and flushes out trapped contaminants.
  3. Regeneration. Injected air naturally recharges the media, with no salt or chemicals.
  4. Fast rinse. The filter bed is re-packed and ready for the next cycle.
  • Removes up to 20 ppm of combined iron, manganese, hydrogen sulfide, and tannins
  • Filters sediment down to 3 microns
  • Stops rust stains and protects fixtures, appliances, and plumbing
  • Low maintenance, with natural media that lasts roughly 5 to 10 years
  • Space-saving single-tank design

Drinking Water

Elevated Drinking System

A five-stage reverse osmosis system that purifies your water, then adds healthy minerals back for cleaner, better-tasting water at the tap.

Five Stages of Purification

  1. Sediment pre-filter. Captures sand, silt, and rust down to 5 microns.
  2. Carbon block. Reduces chlorine, taste, and odor.
  3. Reverse osmosis membrane. Removes dissolved contaminants down to 0.0001 micron.
  4. Remineralization filter. Adds calcium and magnesium back and balances pH.
  5. Polishing carbon post-filter. A final pass for crisp, clean taste.
  • Quiet, water-efficient non-electric permeate pump
  • 3-gallon storage tank for water on demand
  • Quick-change filters (about yearly) and membrane (about every two years)
  • Great for drinking, coffee, tea, and ice

How it works

Look inside the Black Diamond

Open the tank up and see what each part does, from the carbon at the top down to the distributor at the bottom.

Step 1 of 6

Outside

One tank, one job

The Black Diamond is a single tank in a black jacket with a metered control valve on top. It plumbs into the main line where water enters the house, so every tap downstream gets filtered water. Built in the USA and backed by a lifetime warranty.

Step 2 of 6

Look inside

A deep bed of media

Cut the tank open and it is mostly media. A deep bed of activated carbon fills the upper two thirds, resting on a gravel underbedding that keeps the bed level and stops media washing out.

Step 3 of 6

Carbon media

Coconut shell or catalytic

The carbon is the part doing the work. Coconut shell carbon handles chlorine, taste and odor. Catalytic carbon is the choice when your utility uses chloramine, which ordinary carbon struggles with. Available in 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 cubic foot beds.

Step 4 of 6

Gravel underbedding

Keeps the bed where it belongs

Under the carbon sits a layer of graded gravel. It spreads the flow evenly across the bottom of the bed so water cannot channel down one side, and it stops fine media reaching the distributor.

Step 5 of 6

Riser and distributor

How the water travels

Water enters at the top, works down through the carbon, and is collected by the distributor basket at the bottom of the riser tube. It then travels back up the riser and out to the house.

Step 6 of 6

Control valve

Backwashes only when needed

The metered valve on top counts the water you actually use. It backwashes the bed on a schedule driven by that number rather than a timer, which lifts packed media, flushes out what the carbon has caught, and resets the bed.
